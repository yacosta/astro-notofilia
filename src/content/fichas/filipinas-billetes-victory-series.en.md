## Historical and issue context

**Identification:** Philippines (Commonwealth) — Treasury Certificates — Victory Series No. 66 (ND 1944) — Pick 94a (1 peso, Mabini) and Pick 95a (2 pesos, Rizal).

The notes on this record are paper of the Treasury of the Philippines, not Central Bank issues. Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas records that American liberation forces brought Victory Series No. 66 in 1944, and that when sovereignty was recovered the paper in use was Treasury certificates overprinted with the word Victory. The Commonwealth peso was tied to the United States dollar at 2:1.

In 1949, after the Central Bank of the Philippines was created, remaining denominations of that series were overprinted “Central Bank of the Philippines” and stayed legal tender until 30 July 1964. Wikipedia, as a secondary source, notes that the first notes issued by the BSP were those 1949 VICTORY-CBP overprints on American-era paper.

The United States-period background is longer. Wikipedia (American Period) records that in 1903 the Insular Government issued Silver Certificates of 2, 5 and 10 pesos, backed at ₱2/$1, “By Authority of an Act of the Congress of the United States of America, approved March 2, 1903.” In 1918 those silver certificates were replaced by Treasury Certificates (1, 2, 5, 10, 20, 50, 100 and 500 pesos). The BSP agrees on the core point: from May 1918 Treasury certificates replaced the silver-certificate series and a one-peso note was added. The Commonwealth issued Treasury Certificates with the seal of the new government.

> **Important note:** This record documents two specimens **without** the red Central Bank overprint. They are not Pick 117 / Pick 118.

## Design and visible details

Both certificates carry “VICTORY SERIES NO. 66” in place of a date, a blue Commonwealth of the Philippines / United States of America seal, and a black VICTORY overprint across the reverse. Printer: U.S. Bureau of Engraving and Printing, with no imprint on these specimens.

## Known varieties

Series 66 covered several denominations. This record documents only two types **without** the CBP overprint:

- Pick 94a (1 peso, Mabini, Osmeña–Hernandez) — specimen F70618009.
- Pick 95a (2 pesos, Rizal, Auditor General title) — specimen F13317943.

Pick 117 (1 peso) and Pick 118 (2 pesos) are the same design with a red CENTRAL BANK OF THE PHILIPPINES overprint, applied in 1949 on remaining Treasury stock. They are not these specimens. Other signatures, star replacements, specimens, and the rest of the series denominations: unconfirmed in this collection.

## Shown specimen state

Two unencapsulated notes, photographed obverse (top) and reverse (bottom). The 1 peso is shown in clear sleeves; the 2 pesos on a white background. Serials F70618009 and F13317943; blue seals and serials; black VICTORY overprint; no red CBP overprint. Numeric grade: unconfirmed.
